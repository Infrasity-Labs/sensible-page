Why contracts are hard

Why contracts break standard extraction tools

Long-form legal text, nested clauses, and formatting that changes with every counterparty make contracts a language problem, not a layout one.

01

Legal language parsing

Contracts mix structured data (party names, dates, amounts) with dense legal prose. LLM reasoning interprets the legal language; SenseML rules extract the specific fields and values your contract management system needs.

02

Clause identification

Termination, indemnification, confidentiality, force majeure: these clauses appear in varying sections with different numbering schemes. Each clause type is located by semantic meaning, not position, because every firm structures contracts differently.

03

Amendment and addendum linking

Original agreement numbers, modified clauses, new terms: amendments reference their parent contract and change specific provisions. Each modification is extracted as a structured change record linked to the original.

Common questions

Questions from legal and ops teams

Amendments, clause identification, human review, security, and pricing.

Can Sensible handle contract amendments and addenda?+

Yes. Sensible processes amendments as standalone documents, extracting the original agreement reference, amendment date, modified clauses, and new terms. Each modification comes back as a structured change record linked to its parent contract.

Does Sensible extract key dates and terms from contracts?+

Effective date, expiration date, renewal terms, notice periods, payment terms, and milestone dates come back by default. Contracts vary widely, so these are examples of what is commonly pulled, not a closed list. The dates and terms you extract are defined in configuration and shaped to your agreement types.

What party information does Sensible extract from contracts?+

Sensible captures party names, roles (buyer, seller, licensor, licensee), addresses, and signatory details including name, title, and signature date. Roles and party fields are defined in configuration, so you extend them for the agreement types you handle rather than mapping to a fixed set.

Can Sensible identify specific clauses in contracts?+

Yes. Sensible locates clause types like indemnification, limitation of liability, termination, confidentiality, non-compete, and force majeure. Those are common examples. The clauses it looks for are defined in configuration, so you add the provisions specific to your contracts when something is not already covered.

Does Sensible support human review?+

Yes. Sensible flags extractions with low confidence for human review. You can configure review thresholds and workflows.

How is contract data secured, and how long is it retained?+

Sensible is SOC 2 Type II certified and HIPAA compliant. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Document data is stored indefinitely by default; custom retention policies are available and can be configured for same-day deletion if needed.

What file formats does Sensible support?+

Sensible processes PDFs (native or scanned), Microsoft Word (DOC, DOCX), spreadsheets (XLSX, XLS, CSV), single-page images (JPEG, PNG), multi-page images (TIFF), and email bodies with attachments.

How accurate and how fast is contract extraction?+

Accuracy depends on document quality and configuration. Most production deployments achieve 95%+ accuracy with proper validation rules and confidence signals. Processing speed depends on document size, page count, OCR requirements, and which extraction methods are used. Simple single-page documents process in seconds; larger documents that use LLM-based extraction take longer.

How is pricing structured, and is there a free trial?+

Sensible uses per-document pricing for predictable costs, with no token-based billing or usage surprises. Volume discounts are available for higher throughput. There is a 14-day free trial on the Growth plan, and no credit card is required to start.
